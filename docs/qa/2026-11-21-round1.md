# QA round 1 on zawed-dev (2026-11-21)

Four testers (Opus agents) used the live dev environment at schema 22, before the U1 revamp, one per group of roles. Scripts and screenshots stay outside the repository (`wt/qa/round1/`). Nothing below includes credentials.

## Scores (1–10)

| Role | Score | Main reasons |
|---|---|---|
| Public visitor (landing) | 8 | Clear value in Egyptian Arabic, strong form. Missing trust signals, blank areas while scroll reveals run |
| Super Admin | 7 | Clear navigation. No home overview, raw audit codes, no session or MFA management |
| Ops | 6 | Good empty states and import. Money display errors, no contact validation, nav items the role cannot use |
| Ops Manager | 6.5 | Clear approve and reject dialogs. Default view is not the manager's queue; approver names hidden |
| Accountant | 5 | Wallet and top-ups clear. No statement filter or export, no notification when a top-up is credited |
| Station Manager | 5 | Clean and bilingual. No home page, passive finance, device and agent health invisible |
| Fleet Manager | 4 | No dashboard; budgets, limits and reports are placeholders; no vehicle history or limit usage |
| POS agent | 4 | Large targets. No verify and confirm steps; stuck on the PIN-change screen |

Landing page against competitors (tester's scores): ZAWED leads on clarity, Egyptian identity, Arabic quality and motion; trails on trust (no social proof, no real product screenshots) and its placeholder prices are about three times PetroApp's.

## Fixed already

- **High, security:** the API saw every visitor as one Railway-internal address (100.64.x), so per-address limits (sign-in, demo form, POS activation) were shared by everyone. Caddy now trusts `100.64.0.0/10` (`5e7857f`).
- **Low:** the CSP blocked inlined fonts (`font-src` now allows `data:`).

### API, fixed on `local/r1-api` (the web still has to use the new fields)

- **Fractional piastres and millilitres** are refused, not truncated: `100.5` (also `100.0`, `1e2`) in any integer field answers `400 VALIDATION_FAILED` with `fields: [{field, code: "INTEGER_REQUIRED"}]` (`fueling/WholeNumberTests`).
- **Role before body:** a caller without the permission gets `403 FORBIDDEN` before the body is read or validated, on every endpoint (`platform/security/PermissionFirst`, `AuthorizationMatrixTests.aRoleWithoutThePermissionIsRefusedBeforeTheBodyIsRead`).
- **Contact validation (API half):** company and operator `contactPhone` (Egyptian mobile or landline), `taxId` (nine digits, dashes accepted), `commercialRegistration` (3–12 digits) and agent `phone` (Egyptian mobile) are validated and stored cleaned (`tenancy/CompanyContactTests`, `NetworkTests`). The web forms still need their own checks.
- **Plate search** treats `ه` and `هـ` alike (`FleetTests.plateSearchIgnoresTheTatweel`). The duplicate check still does not: `known-issues.md`.
- **Maker-checker names (API half):** top-ups, wallet adjustments, bank changes and payouts carry `…ByName` and `…ByRole`; tenants see ZAWED staff only as `ZAWED_OPERATIONS`. Top-ups carry `receivingAccountLabel` when one is configured (`ledger/MakerCheckerNamesTests`, `StationFinanceTests`).
- **Device and agent health (API half):** `DeviceResponse.activationExpired` and `AgentResponse.locked` (with `mustChangePin`, `lockedUntil`) for Ops and the station manager (`NetworkTests`).
- **Leads note limit:** the API allows 1,000 characters (JavaScript `string.length`, before trimming). The web's 500 is the bug.
- **Station bank-account change request:** not built. ADR 0020 §8 and the B6 contract give the request to Ops; the roadmap delta §G suggests "station requests, Ops approves". Owner decision pending (b6 contract §6).

## Open, by area

### Security and API
- ~~Fractional piastres are truncated (`100.5` → 100) instead of refused with 400.~~ Fixed in the API (above).
- ~~Some Ops endpoints validate the body before checking the role (a station token gets 400 with field names, not 403).~~ Fixed (above).

- The Super Admin signs in with a password only on zawed-dev (`ZAWED_MFA_REQUIRED=false`, owner's choice) and the password is below policy. Restore MFA before any real use.

### Money and data display
- The company checklist shows the wallet in raw piastres (`1000075`).
- Limits and budgets are rounded to whole pounds (5000.50 → 5,001).
- Maker-checker cards show "Verified by 01a1f8dd" (an id) and never the approver; "Receiving account MAIN" is a raw code. API now sends names and the label (above); web open.
- The checklist says "Company is active" while the company is frozen.
- Dates without a year; mixed date formats.

### Missing screens and services
- No home overview for the company, the station or Ops (each "Home" redirects to a list).
- Company budgets, limits and reports are placeholders; vehicle detail has no fueling history or limit usage.
- No notification bell (the API exists since W5).
- Statement cannot be filtered or exported; no monthly statement.
- Tickets cannot be linked to a vehicle or fueling from the UI.
- Station manager cannot request a bank-account change (by design today, owner decision pending, above); no payout schedule.
- Devices with expired activation codes show "awaiting activation"; locked agents show "active". API now says so (above); web open.

### POS
- No verify step (plate, wallet status, limit left) before the volume, and no confirm step with the amount.
- The forced PIN-change screen has no way out; the shift stays open.
- The simulator needs the full tag UID; seeded UIDs are unknown. A dev-only tag picker is needed for demos and QA.
- No language switch, history or end-of-shift summary; lockout message never counts down.

### Landing page
- No social proof or product screenshots; scroll reveals leave blank areas; no Open Graph tags, `robots.txt` or `sitemap.xml`; no privacy policy or terms; `/demo` launcher in the public footer does not work against the API.
- Accessibility: language button name differs from its text; Escape does not close the mobile menu; footer links under 24 px.

### Smaller
- Status filters overflow at 390 px (fixed in the revamp branch), naming differs between nav and page titles, "Back to the demo" on the not-found page, unknown `/ops/*` paths redirect silently, the bank self-approve button is shown, the leads note limit says 500 (contract 1,000), a scheduled price cannot be cancelled.

### Test environment
- Seeded activation codes expire 15 minutes after seeding and all agents must change their PIN first, so fueling could not be tested end to end. Round 2 issues fresh codes and known tag UIDs through Ops.
- The web host stalled on about 1 in 5 requests during the run; not reproduced afterwards (20 of 20 under 1.4 s).
