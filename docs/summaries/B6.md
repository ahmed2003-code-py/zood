# Phase B6 summary: station finance, tickets, exports and the activity calendar

**Date:** 2026-11-19 · **Branch:** `develop`
**Contract:** `docs/architecture/b6-station-finance-contract.md` · **ADR:** 0020 · **Schema:** `V9__station_finance.sql`, `V10__shifts_visible_to_operators.sql`, `V11__tickets.sql`

## What was done

- **Station payables.** Each station's payable has a cached balance under its own lock, as wallets do. Reconciliation now checks payables too, and a payable may go below zero after a reversal.
- **Bank accounts under change control.** One person requests a change with the bank letter's reference, and a different person approves it. The account number is encrypted and only its last four characters are shown. Approval starts a 72-hour hold before any payout.
- **Payouts.**
  - A payout never exceeds *balance − held − pending*.
  - Above a threshold (default 50,000 EGP) it waits for a second person.
  - One bank reference pays once.
- **Held offline payables** wait until Operations releases them, with a reason.
- **Two-person reversals** post the exact mirror of the fueling. The fueling row stays immutable.
- **Dual control in the database:** CHECK constraints refuse a decision by the person who asked.
- **Station portal (read-only):** payable, statement and summary, masked bank account, posted payouts, approved reversals. Companies see approved reversals.
- **Station managers sign in with a second factor.**
- **Support tickets** for companies and stations, answered by Ops. Ops can write internal notes, links are checked for visibility, and each side can write only through its own portal.
- **CSV exports** of fuelings for each portal (Excel-friendly, formula injection defused).
- **Activity calendar:** a month view per platform, company, vehicle, station, device or agent. Choosing a day opens that day's fuelings.
- **Quick period filters** (today, this week, last week, this month, last month), plus shifts for Ops and station managers.
