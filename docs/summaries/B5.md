## What was done

- **Snapshot** (`GET /pos/snapshot`, device only). The terminal downloads it to decide offline. It carries:
  - the station and its price schedule;
  - tags named by a 32-bit digest under a per-device key (no UIDs; 4-byte UIDs left out);
  - vehicles, and limits as what remains in the period;
  - each company's wallet as a status word and an allowance capped at 5,000 EGP and rounded to 100 EGP.

  It carries no PIN hashes. A contract version header answers `426` to terminals that must update.
- **Batch sync** (`POST /pos/sync`, device only):
  - Up to 100 captures, each in its own transaction, idempotent on `clientTxnId`.
  - A fueling for a known tag is always recorded at its own time, `COMPLETED`, with every failing check kept as a conflict and alerted.
  - What the wallet cannot cover goes to a company receivable account. The station is credited in full and the wallet never goes negative.
  - A server-side bound on offline spending per device and company holds the payable and raises a critical alert.
  - A fault on one item answers `RETRY`.
- **Lost online answers.** The terminal saves each attempt before sending it and resolves it later (`POST /pos/fuelings/{id}/resolve`). A fueling the server completed while the agent was told it was refused raises `OUTCOME_MISMATCH`.
- **Sync monitor** for Operations and station managers.
- **Swagger UI** at `/api/v1/docs`, with every endpoint and a working Authorize.
- **Web POS:**
  - Decides offline in the server's order, on the server's clock, against its snapshot plus its own captures.
  - Persistent queue in IndexedDB. Refused captures are set aside with an export for Operations.
  - Syncs when the network returns, also after the shift. Has a "simulate no network" switch.
- **Ops screens:** a terminal-sync screen, and offline conflicts shown on fuelings.
